# Moscow & Muscovites

## Author
Vladimir Gilyaroski is a jounralist and socil figure who lived in Moscow at the beginning of the 20th century.

## About the project
To pay tribute to the author and his work documenting the daily life of Muscovites, we embarked on the journey of updating this piece of journalism work to the digital age.

## Using the map

The map displays 3 main types of information.

### Places

Along his journey through the lively streets of Moscow, the author mentions important landmarks of the era across most of the city centre.

### Sentiment

His descriptions often contain curious descriptions of people and social context of the place, this is useful in extracting a general sentiment of a place.

### Route

Chapter 2 of the book is entirely dedicated to the description of his journey across the city centre, we attempt to map this journey and the key witnessed along the way.
